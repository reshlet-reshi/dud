# Beautiful Policy Policy

Policy exists to reduce uncertainty.

Policy is not a place to perform seriousness. It is a tool for making good
decisions repeatable with less negotiation, less memory, and less fear.

This policy follows a simple test. If a rule does not prevent real harm,
preserve real value, or make action easier, remove it.

## Principles

Prefer fewer rules with clearer force.

Prefer examples over abstractions.

Prefer local judgment over remote ceremony.

Prefer visible tradeoffs over hidden intent.

Prefer repairable mistakes over elaborate prevention.

Prefer policies that can be read by the people bound by them.

## Rules

A policy must say what to do.

A policy must say when it applies.

A policy must not pretend to cover cases it cannot understand.

A policy must not duplicate another rule unless the duplication prevents a
specific, recurring mistake.

A policy must not require a private interpreter.

## Checks

Can a tired person follow it?

Can a new person find the rule that matters?

Can a reasonable exception be made without breaking trust?

Can obsolete text be removed without ceremony?

Does the policy make action lighter than confusion would?

## Source Spirit

The beautiful document minimizes policy by making its own claims testable. It
does not need a governance system to explain what it is doing.
