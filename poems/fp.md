## `Explaining from first principles` means:

- explaining an idea as if unnamed.

### To `Explain from first priciples`:

1. Strip away:
    - inherited terms,
    - analogies,
    - and assumptions;
- until only
    - the raw problem,
    - and unavoidable constraints,
    - remain.

2. Rebuild the idea,
    - layer by layer,
    - so it feels necessary,
    - rather than memorized.

3. Ask the idea:
    - what problem it solves,
    - what fails without it,
    - what tradeoffs it makes,
    - and, "_must_ you exist as you do."

4. Attend to:
    - cost,
    - failure,
    - capacity,
    - the unexpected,
    - and the hidden.

5. Introduce jargon only when
    - the need for it has been earned.

6. End with the technical definition.
